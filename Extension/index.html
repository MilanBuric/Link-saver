Link Saver

Used only for "Save current tab". Get a free key at tinyurl.com/app/dev.

New links are sorted into categories by domain rules first (free, instant). If a link doesn't match any rule and a key is set here, it's sent to Gemini to pick or suggest a category. Get a free key at aistudio.google.com (starts with "AIzaSy"). Stored on this device only.

Mirrors your links and categories through Chrome's built-in sync storage so they show up on your other signed-in browsers. That storage is small (~100KB total) — fine for most lists, but very large collections may not fully fit, in which case sync fails quietly and your local copy stays safe either way. Must be turned on separately on each device/browser you want synced.
